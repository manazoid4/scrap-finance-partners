- Confirm professional/regulatory boundaries

- Add client reporting and document exchange to the working acquisition CRM
- Create reporting upload workflow
- Package dashboard/reporting into repeatable system
- Test Scrap Finance OS concept

- Monthly retainer base
- Repeatable diagnostic product
- Strong LinkedIn authority
- Partnerships with accountants, consultants and recycling networks
- Client portal
- Reporting automation
- Potential SaaS-lite product
