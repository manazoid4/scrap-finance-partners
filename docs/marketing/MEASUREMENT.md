# Measurement

Funnel: visitor → Health Check CTA → valid lead → qualified lead → conversation → proposal → won work.

A qualified lead includes a company and declared commercial pain. Use non-PII fields only for analytics: offer, placement, page, intent, campaign, content and device class. Names, emails, companies, phones and free text must never enter analytics.

UTM convention: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`, `utm_term`. Revenue and sales-stage reporting remains in the approved private CRM or pipeline, not public analytics. Do not add advertising pixels or persistent attribution without a separate privacy/consent review.
