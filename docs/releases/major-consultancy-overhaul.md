# Major consultancy-first overhaul

Status: Released to production on 29 July 2026, subject to the approval gates below for any later claim or campaign activation.

## Objective

Make the Finance Health Check the clearest commercial entry point; repair mobile reflow; publish the supplied margin case study faithfully; and create a safe operating scaffold for onboarding, changelogs and future marketing.

## Included

- Shared responsive header/footer and one consultancy-first visual system.
- Health Check, services, ways-to-work, case study, insights and updates journeys.
- YardLedger labelled Exploring and removed from indexed primary navigation.
- Qualified lead capture with non-PII attribution in the internal notification.
- Claims, onboarding and campaign templates.

## Excluded

CRM, advertising pixels, ad spend, automatic publishing, payment, a client portal, fabricated proof and regulated advice.

## Gates for later activation

Written client approval is required separately for identity/credentials, claims and prices, public contact routing, tracking/consent and paid media. Roll back by reverting this release PR and redeploying `master`.
