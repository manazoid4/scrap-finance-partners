# Industrial editorial corrective release

Status: Production candidate.

## Design decision

Use the shared research brief’s “Industrial Editorial Brutalism” direction with restraint: rigorous typography, hard structural rules, asymmetry, purposeful density and copper reserved for action.

## Deliberate exclusions

- No invented asset-finance or lending services.
- No “financial engineering” or high-net-worth claims.
- No unverified market data or macroeconomic statistics.
- No stock, generated or staged industrial imagery. Authentic client imagery may be introduced after approval.
- No gradients, shadows, rounded cards, decorative grids, animation theatre or sticky mobile conversion obstruction.

## Preserved commercial foundations

The Finance Health Check remains primary. The client-supplied margin case study remains evidence-safe. YardLedger remains Exploring and non-indexed. Lead qualification and attribution remain unchanged.
