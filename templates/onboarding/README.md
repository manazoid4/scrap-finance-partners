# Client onboarding pack

1. Complete the client intake.
2. Supply evidence for every intended claim.
3. Approve positioning, offer scope, fees and timing.
4. Approve public identity, contact channels and form recipients.
5. Approve privacy, analytics and consent choices.
6. Review the Vercel production candidate and sign the launch checklist.

Production release and paid-media activation are separate approvals. Store completed packs privately; these repository files are blank templates only.
